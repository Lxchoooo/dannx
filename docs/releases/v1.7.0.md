## DannX 1.7.0

### Filter the feed by tags

- New tag chips on Home: choose one or several and the feed shows the wallpapers
  that use any of them.
- A tag written in capitals or lowercase now counts as the same tag, so a single
  chip covers both spellings.
- Tags with spaces, commas or symbols work like any other tag.
- Your choice stays when you move between screens and comes back.

### Stability

- Small fixes and maintenance under the hood.
