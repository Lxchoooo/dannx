## DannX 1.6.0

### Updates from the app

- **Check for updates in Settings.** DannX can now find a new version, download it and open the Android installer without leaving the app.
- If something goes wrong, the app explains what happened and lets you open the release page.

### A faster, lighter feed

- The feed loads **30 wallpapers at a time** and keeps loading as you scroll, instead of downloading the whole catalogue at once.
- Search, category filters and tags now cover the whole catalogue, not only what was already downloaded.
- The offline cache keeps up to **400 wallpapers** and preserves the ones you already had.

### Surprise me

- A new **Surprise me** chip opens a random wallpaper from the current search or filter, avoiding the one you just saw.

### Accessibility

- Better screen reader labels and states on wallpaper cards and favourite buttons.
- Larger touch targets and more comfortable text scaling in the main screens.
- Smoother right-to-left layout.
