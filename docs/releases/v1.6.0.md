## DannX 1.6.0

### Updates from the app

- **Check for updates in Settings.** DannX now looks up the latest release,
  downloads it inside the app and verifies its SHA-256 checksum before opening
  the Android installer.
- If a download fails or the checksum does not match, the app explains what
  happened and offers to open the release page instead.

### A faster, lighter feed

- The feed now loads **30 wallpapers at a time** and appends the next page as
  you scroll, instead of downloading the whole catalogue at once.
- Search, category filters and tags now query the server, so results are not
  limited to what was already downloaded.
- The offline cache keeps up to **400 wallpapers**, merging new pages with the
  ones you already had.

### Surprise me

- A new **Surprise me** chip opens a random wallpaper from the current search
  or filter, avoiding the one you just saw.

### Accessibility

- Better TalkBack labels and states for wallpaper cards and favourite buttons.
- Larger touch targets and improved text scaling in the main screens.
- More consistent right-to-left icon behaviour.
